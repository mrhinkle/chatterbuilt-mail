# Email memory

Copy this file to `memory/EMAIL-MEMORY.md` (or into your Google Drive or OneDrive folder) and fill it in. Plain sentences are fine. Claude reads it whenever it sets up or improves your Chatterbuilt Mail, and the improve skill adds what the app learns.

If this file holds anything personal, keep it in a private repo or folder.

## Who I am
- Name, role, company, and the one or two things I spend my email time on.
- My main address, and other addresses I forward copies to.

## People who matter
- VIPs who always get a fast, personal reply (name, who they are, how I talk to them).
- Assistants or teammates I loop in, and when.

## How I write
- Tone in one line (for example "warm, brief, a little dry").
- How I open and sign off.
- Words or phrases I never use. Spelling preferences.
- Where my longer voice guide lives (a Notion page link), if I have one.

## Where things go
- Folders and labels I use, and what belongs in each.
- My task app and the list or project tasks should land in, plus any fields my view needs to show them.
- My contacts app or CRM, and what I want saved about people.

## Rules
- Standing rules in plain words, for example "Receipts and invoices go to Finance" or "Mail from stripe.com: archive".
- Things I always want flagged, never archived.

## Never
- Things the assistant must never do (for example "never decline a speaking invitation without asking me").
